The ICC: How Much Lies Between Schools

Of all the differences in children’s reading scores, how much lies between schools?
Most differences are among children in the same school, but about a fifth lie between schools.

  1. 10% of teachers are satisfied
  2. Schools explain 90% of the differences
  3. The survey is 10% accurate
  4. About 10% of the differences lie between schools

Why Ordinary Regression Is Too Sure of Itself

Ordinary regression counts every child as a separate, independent piece of information.
Children in the same school partly repeat each other, because they share so much.
So results look more certain than they really are: the standard errors come out too small.

Random Effects in One Picture

Each school gets its own starting point. We still estimate one overall relationship.

In Stata

mixed x2rscalk1 x12sesl || s2_id:
estat icc

|| s2_id: tells Stata children are grouped in schools.
The SES coefficient (5.2) reads like regular regression.

PlayPosit Question 3

In mixed x2rscalk1 x12sesl || s2_id:, what does || s2_id: do?

  1. Tells Stata children are grouped within schools
  2. Drops schools with missing data
  3. Adds school ID as a regular predictor
  4. Standardizes the reading score

When You Read a Study

When you read a study
The same idea goes by several names: multilevel models, hierarchical linear models (HLM), mixed models, random effects.

Ask: What is grouped within what? How much lies between the groups?
